The problem

Job boards match keywords. Your resume says more than that.

Keyword filters

You search by title, scroll through hundreds of listings, guess which ones fit, and find out about missing skills only after you have applied.

A ranked shortlist

JobMatch reads your whole resume, searches by meaning and by exact words, orders the best results, and tells you why each one fits.

Benefits

From resume to a shortlist you can check

See the best fits first

Each search runs three ways: by meaning, by words in the description, and by words in the title. A language model then reorders the top 50.

Know why a job fits

Every match comes with a short explanation and a fit score out of 100, written from your resume and the posting.

See the exact skills to add

Matched and missing skills come from the posting itself, so the gap list is never guessed by a model.

Teach it your taste

Like or dislike a match and your next results move toward what you liked. Jobs you dislike stay hidden.

Ask instead of filtering

Say only remote ones, or compare the second one with my resume. The agent remembers the conversation and shows every tool it used.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Is JobMatch free?
Yes. You can create an account and use every feature without a card.
Which resumes work?
Text based PDFs up to 5 MB. Scanned images are not supported, because there is no text to read.
Are these live job openings?
No. The index comes from a public dataset of LinkedIn postings from April 2024, and it grows in small batches every six hours. Use it to see how the matching works, not to apply.
How are matches ranked?
Three searches run together: one by meaning, one on the posting text, and one on the title. Their results are merged, and a language model reorders the top 50.
What happens to my resume?
The text is stored in the app database so matches can be computed, and only your account can read it. To extract your profile, the text is sent to the Google Gemini API.
Can I trust the explanations?
The skill gap comes from a skills list matched against the posting. The explanation and the fit score are written by a language model, so they can be wrong. Treat them as a starting point.
What can the chat agent do?
It can search jobs, open a job, compare it with your resume, save it, and list your saved jobs. Each reply shows the tools it called.
Does it learn from my feedback?
Yes. Likes pull your next results toward similar jobs. Dislikes push them away and hide that job.
Why is it sometimes slow or busy?
The demo runs on free model quotas. If a model is busy you will see a short message. Wait a minute and try again.
Can I read the code?
Yes. The project is open source on GitHub, including the evaluation suite.
