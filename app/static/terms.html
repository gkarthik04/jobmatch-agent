What this is

JobMatch is a portfolio project that shows resume parsing, job ranking, and a tool using agent. It is provided as is, without any warranty, and it may be slow, change, or go offline without notice.

Archived and open postings

Most postings come from a public dataset of LinkedIn listings from April 2024. Those listings have closed and are for demonstrating the matching. Open jobs come from the Arbeitnow feed. Apply links open third party sites that we do not control, and we cannot guarantee that a job is still available.

Machine generated content can be wrong

Profiles, explanations, fit scores, and chat replies are produced by a language model. Check anything that matters before you act on it.

Acceptable use

  • Upload only your own resume, or one you have permission to use.
  • Do not upload sensitive personal information.
  • Do not try to overload, probe, or break the service.

Limits

The demo runs on free model quotas, so requests are rate limited and can fail when the models are busy. See the privacy page for how your data is handled.
